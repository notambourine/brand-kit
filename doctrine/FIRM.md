# NoTambourine's firm: who we are

## Firm

NoTambourine · a boutique AI-native engineering agency. It puts senior engineers
inside your team to lead delivery. They take your direction, work from inside
your repositories, communication channels, and operating cadence, and improve
that process as part of the engagement. NoTambourine plans each project around
the business result it should produce. After its most recent redesign launched,
traffic rose 20% and conversion rate rose 20% on top of it.

The work may be a platform migration, a redesign, site speed, or ongoing
engineering. It may also launch a product, automate routine work, or connect
systems and data. The team trains on AI and sets up CI/CD so a small team ships
daily. The [board](https://notambourine.com/velocity) shows the pace.

Fit depends on the result you need and the responsibility NoTambourine takes on,
whatever your ownership structure. The firm has private-equity experience when
that context is relevant. An engagement may start with a new ambition or an
established constraint.

Start a conversation by sharing what you want to accomplish and when. We shape
the engagement and terms around how your organization already buys and works.

The tagline is "Engineering you can see in the numbers."

Tom Fuertes is the principal. He has 20 years of organizational-change
experience across more than 250 engagements.

## Names and citations

Use one of three wordmark forms. Write `NoTambourine` in every human-facing
sentence. Lowercase `notambourine` is the technical slug, correct only in a
path, URL, domain, GitHub org, npm name, or CSS class. `NoTambourine LLC` is the
legal entity and appears twice at most in a contract, the signature block and
one Definitions anchor, and nowhere else. `Notambourine`, `No Tambourine`, and
any other spaced or split form, including wordplay on the instrument, are not
valid.

[SOUL.md](SOUL.md) says what we believe, for clients. [CULTURE.md](CULTURE.md)
holds the team's internal working rules. The site publishes edited versions of
both, so anything missing from them says nothing about unpublished policy.

The site publishes no client list, case studies, rates, or margin data.
References, commercial terms, and engagement details come through a direct
conversation.
